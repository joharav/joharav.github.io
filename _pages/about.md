<div style="text-align: justify;">
I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Michigan. My research studies how households and firms respond to institutional frictions such as multi-currency pricing and innovation incentives. I combine quantitative macro models with micro data and behavioral methods.

Previously, I worked in the Monetary Policy Division of the Central Bank of Peru and studied Economics at the Universidad del Pacífico.
