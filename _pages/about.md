**Fields**: Macroeconomics, Behavioral Economics, International Economics.

<div style="text-align: justify;">
I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Michigan. My research studies how households and firms allocate resources in multi-currency environments, with a focus on durable consumption, exchange-rate exposure, and transactional dollarization. I combine computational macroeconomics with behavioral and experimental methods.

<br><br>

Before Michigan, I worked at the Central Bank of Peru. I earned my BA in Economics from Universidad del Pacifico in 2018.
