**Fields**: Macroeconomics, International Economics, Computational Economics.

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the University of Michigan.  
My research studies how households and firms make decisions in multi-currency environments, with a focus on dollar invoicing, durable adjustment, and policy design in emerging economies.  
I use quantitative macro models, micro data, and behavioral/experimental methods.

- **Welfare Effects of Dollar Currency Invoicing: The Case of Durable Goods**  
  <details>
    <summary>Abstract</summary>
    How does dollar pricing of durable goods affect household welfare in small open economies? This project extends a lumpy adjustment framework by adding exchange-rate exposure at replacement. The model implies delayed adjustment for some households and heterogeneous welfare effects.
  </details>
  Draft available upon request.
