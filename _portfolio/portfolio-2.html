This line of work evaluates policies designed to reduce transactional dollarization in small open economies.

  • Status: published in Journal of Economic Behavior and Organization (2022), with ongoing extensions.
  • Methods: laboratory experiments and model-based policy analysis.
  • Policy angle: taxes on foreign-currency transactions and incentives for local-currency use.
